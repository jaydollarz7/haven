Who We Are

ICE FIXIT LLC

ICE FIXIT is a technology company based in Trenton, New Jersey. We work with small and mid-size businesses that need a reliable technology partner — someone who can fix what's broken, protect what matters, and automate what's slowing them down.

We're not a help desk, a break-fix shop, or a software vendor. We're a technology partner — which means we're thinking about your whole situation, not just the ticket in front of us.

Everything we do is grounded in practical, real-world experience. We've seen what works in actual business environments, what gets ignored when theory meets reality, and how to give business owners straight answers without overselling or overcomplicating things.

Our Philosophy

How We Think About Technology

Technology should serve the business — not the other way around. That belief shapes every recommendation we make.

🗣️

Plain English, always

We won't hide behind jargon. If we can't explain something clearly, we don't understand it well enough to be recommending it.

🎯

Practical, not theoretical

Our recommendations come from solving real problems in real businesses — not certifications, vendor partnerships, or what looked good in a case study.

🤝

Honest about trade-offs

Every technology decision has trade-offs. We'll tell you what they are — including when the answer is "you don't actually need this."

📍

Local accountability

We're in Trenton. Our clients are in Trenton. That proximity creates a kind of accountability that a remote-only provider can't replicate.

🔮

Prevention over firefighting

We'd rather prevent problems than fix them. After every engagement, we look at what caused the issue and how to stop it from recurring.

⚖️

Right-sized solutions

The best tool is the one that fits your budget, your team, and your actual workflow — not the most sophisticated or the most popular.
