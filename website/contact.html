Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The 30-minute consultation is completely free and comes with no obligation. Our goal is to understand your situation and give you useful information — not to close a sale on the first call.

What if I don't know what service I need?

That's perfectly fine. Select "General Consultation" in the form and describe what's happening. We'll figure out the right path together during the consultation.

Do you work with businesses outside Trenton?

Yes. We provide on-site support across Mercer County, Princeton, and the Philadelphia area. For clients further away, we offer full remote support for tech support, cybersecurity, and automation.

How quickly can you respond to an urgent issue?

For new inquiries, we respond within one business day. For active clients with urgent issues, we have faster response options available. Tell us in the form if something is time-sensitive.
