The Reality

You Don't Need to Be a Large Company to Be a Target

Most cyberattacks against small businesses aren't sophisticated — they exploit simple gaps that are easy to close once you know about them.

43%

of cyberattacks target small businesses

Small businesses are attractive because they often have less protection and more trust-based relationships that can be exploited.

95%

of breaches involve human error

Most successful attacks don't bypass firewalls — they get an employee to click a link. Training matters as much as software.

60%

of small businesses close within 6 months of a major breach

The financial and reputational damage of a serious incident often exceeds what a small business can absorb. Prevention is far cheaper.

Services Included

What's Covered Under Secure

We focus on the security measures that actually protect small businesses — not the enterprise checkbox exercise.

🔍
Security Risk Assessment

We evaluate your current setup, identify vulnerabilities, and give you a prioritized list of what to fix — starting with what matters most.

🛡️
Endpoint Protection

Business-grade antivirus and threat detection on all your devices — managed and monitored, not just installed and forgotten.

📧
Email Security

Phishing filters, spam protection, and domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) so your email doesn't become an attack vector.

👥
Employee Security Training

Your team is your first line of defense. We train employees to recognize phishing, handle sensitive data, and practice safe habits.

🔑
Password & Access Management

Password policies, multi-factor authentication, and role-based access controls — the basics that most small businesses skip.

📡
Network Security Review

Firewall configuration, Wi-Fi segmentation, remote access security — your network should be locked down, not open to anyone who asks.

💾
Backup & Disaster Recovery

Tested, automated backups — local and cloud. When ransomware hits or a drive fails, you recover in hours, not weeks.

🚨
Incident Response Planning

A clear plan for what to do if something goes wrong — who to call, what to shut down, how to communicate, how to recover.

📋
Compliance Guidance

For industries with regulatory requirements (healthcare, finance, legal) — we help you understand what's required and how to meet it without overcomplicating things.
