About

We didn't start as an AI company.
We started by fixing what was actually broken.

ICE FIXIT began as hands-on technology repair and support work — the kind where you're in front of the actual problem, not theorizing about it from a help desk script. Over time, that practical experience grew into something broader: cybersecurity, technical architecture, and AI automation, all built on the same foundation of "look at what's really happening, then fix it."

Founded by James Scott IV and registered as a New Jersey LLC based in Trenton, ICE FIXIT works with small businesses, contractors, consultants, real estate professionals, and independent professionals across Mercer County, Princeton, and the Philadelphia corridor — plus remote clients nationwide.
